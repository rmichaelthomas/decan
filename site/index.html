01 / Evidence

Meaning becomes a trace, not a guess.

Decan's core is deliberately small. It canonicalizes intent, requires pinned context, resolves only its declared exact subset, and makes a chosen result replayable.

03 / Boundary

Useful because it refuses to pretend.

The core is exact where it says it is, explicit where it is not, and never becomes the authority that acts.

  • No ambient clock, timezone, locale, geolocation, network, or live observer.
  • No authorization, execution, retry, verification, or fulfillment of work.
  • No silent cron or RRULE approximation: adapters return a TemporalLossReport for exact or unsupported conversion.
  • No claim that a timestamp alone contains the meaning someone intended.
04 / Corpus

Three shapes of time that do not fit a naked trigger.

Workflow automation

A recurring product action needs lifecycle origin and a selected timezone, not just five cron fields.

Policy-shaped maintenance

A cadence can depend on windows, suppression, and supplied policy facts. Decan records an honest need when those facts are absent.

Protocol obligations

Deadlines, grace periods, acknowledgements, and expiry are relationships and boundaries, not merely jobs that run later.

05 / Horizon

Open core, long horizon.

The reference core already carries pinned astronomical snapshots for mission-planning cases. Scientific-scale transformation, managed snapshots, audit replay, MCP operations, and connectors remain separate profiles or products, never hidden execution inside the primitive.
