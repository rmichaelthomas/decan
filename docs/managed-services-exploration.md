# Managed Services Exploration

## Open core

Decan remains Apache-2.0, local-first, deterministic, and self-hostable. It is not a scheduler, executor, calendar server, live observer, or authority service. Source, snapshots, resolution, and Occurrence evidence remain portable artifacts.

## Product hypotheses

### Signed Snapshot Registry

Publish versioned timezone, business-calendar, organizational, and scientific-context bundles with source provenance, signatures, validity windows, and content identities. Validate demand by asking whether teams currently maintain these inputs manually and whether a pinned bundle makes replay or compliance easier.

### Evidence Replay Archive

Store the canonical intent, supplied snapshots, resolution identity, derivation, and Occurrence history for long-term retrieval. Validate demand with a replay demonstration for a regulated or incident-review scenario; the archive records evidence and never executes the represented work.

### Managed MCP

Operate a managed Decan endpoint and policy-controlled deployment surface for agent hosts. Validate demand by measuring setup burden, latency tolerance, and whether organizations require centralized access control or usage evidence. The hosted endpoint exposes the same snapshot-only semantics as local MCP.

### Enterprise Connectors

Offer connectors that transform external calendar, policy, and organizational data into signed, versioned snapshots or exact adapter inputs. Validate each connector independently; no connector may cause Decan core to fetch live data during resolution.

## Boundary rule

Commercial value is managed freshness, provenance, retention, operations, and organization integration. Semantic truth remains in the open core, and any approximation must remain visible through a Temporal Loss Report.
