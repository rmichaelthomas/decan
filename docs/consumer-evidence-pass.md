# Post-C6 Consumer Evidence Gate

Decan now has a verified local temporal-core and SQLite durable-occurrence seam. The next work is not automatic: collect real consumer scenarios, their pinned inputs, observed integration limits, and independent reproduction evidence.

Only that evidence may justify hardening, adapters, consumer-specific interfaces, or a package split. Binding, authority, execution, retry, verification, fulfillment, scheduler services, cron/RRULE adapters, live observers, and hidden provider fallbacks remain out of scope.
